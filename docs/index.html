How flags work & their limits. Flags come from transparent keyword rules over each article's own text, not editorial judgement. “Omits” means none of a curated list of climate or accountability terms was found — expand “Why flagged” on any card to see the exact evidence and verify it yourself. Keyword matching is blunt (paywalled articles are marked low-confidence), and this is a rolling ~3-month window from GDELT, not a complete archive.
